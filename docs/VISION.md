# Vision

## What We're Building

An immersive, browser-based open-world western game that captures the essence of what makes the genre compelling: a living world you can wander, a horse that becomes your companion, gunfights that feel legendary, and choices that leave a moral mark.

## Target Experience

A player opens a URL, steps into the dusty frontier of 1899, and within 30 seconds — without any tutorial — understands what kind of game this is. They ride across a sun-baked plain as golden hour light turns the sky amber. A stranger on the roadside calls for help, or doesn't. A year later, they still remember that afternoon.

## Target Users

- Players who value atmosphere and embodiment over fast-paced mechanics
- Fans of open-world western narratives (RDR2, Deadwood, True Grit)
- Casual-to-mid players who want a relaxed but textured experience accessible in a browser

## Success Criteria — Vertical Slice

- [ ] Player can traverse a 1 km² terrain on foot and on horseback without frame drops
- [ ] Day/night cycle and weather system are visibly changing the atmosphere in real time
- [ ] At least one complete encounter arc: bandit ambush → Dead Eye combat → honor consequence
- [ ] The horse follows the player, can be mounted, and gallops convincingly
- [ ] A new player understands the honor/reputation system without reading a manual

## Non-Goals (Post-Slice)

- Multiplayer
- Voice acting or full narrative
- Photorealistic assets
- Mobile / VR support (defer to post-v1)
